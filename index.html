About me

Where cloud engineering meets security engineering.

I started out as a systems administrator, moved into AWS infrastructure automation, and along the way became the person my teams pulled in whenever something needed to be both fast and defensible. Today I split my time between building Infrastructure as Code and hardening the systems it produces.

I care about pipelines that fail loudly and safely, infrastructure that's reproducible from a single command, and security that's built in at design time — not bolted on after an incident. I've led migrations to fully containerized, zero-downtime deployments, and I've also been the one paged at 2 a.m. to trace a suspicious login across three AWS accounts.

Outside of terminals and threat models, you'll usually find me underwater, on two wheels, or being supervised by my dog while I work.
